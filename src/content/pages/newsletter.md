Usually one long essay a month, sometimes less, with a short note about what I am reading and what is on the desk. The test is whether there is anything in the month worth your inbox space, and some months there is not.

There is no schedule and no schedule to keep. When a month has nothing in it, nothing comes out, and the next letter says so. An archive of a writer's site that fills itself with announcements is a newsletter nobody opens.

## What is in it

The letter is one piece, and then a few lines of reading. No news, no offers, no other people's launches, no links to things I have merely skimmed. If something I read was worth the time, I will say what it was and why; if it was not, it does not go in.

There is no sign-up wall in front of any of what has already gone out, and there will not be one. Subscribing is for the next letter, not for these.

## Leaving

One click, from any issue, and I will not take it personally. The address is never sold, never shared, and never handed to anything that tracks. There is no analytics on this site at all, which is the subject of the [colophon](/colophon).
