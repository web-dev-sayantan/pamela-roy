There is an address on this page and a form under it, and they arrive in the same inbox. Use whichever you prefer.

I read everything that comes in, in the order it comes, and I answer most of it. The exceptions are pitches for work I am not the right person for, which I pass on rather than leave hanging, and the occasional one that needs a longer answer than a reply to a form can carry.

## What to expect

A reply from a person, in a few days, occasionally longer if it has been a strange month. If I have nothing useful to say I will say that rather than send something encouraging and empty.

I would rather have a short note than a long one. If you have a book in you, a piece that will not finish, or an interview you would rather do properly than quickly, those are the ones I am glad to hear about.

There is a [newsletter](/newsletter) for the letters, and a [colophon](/colophon) for how any of this is built.
